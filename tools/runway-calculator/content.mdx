This runway calculator shows how many months your cash will last at your current spending. Enter the cash in the bank and your monthly costs, add monthly revenue to see your net burn, and set a target to find the spending cut that gets you there. It works for startups, small businesses and freelancers alike.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose your currency** and enter the **cash in the bank**. The starting values are examples only.
2. **Enter your monthly costs.** This is your gross burn: everything you spend in a typical month.
3. **Add your monthly revenue** if you have any. Leave it at 0 to see runway on costs alone.
4. **Set a target runway** if you have one in mind. Leave it at 0 to skip.

The result shows your runway in months, your gross and net burn, roughly when the cash runs out, and with a target, the monthly cut you'd need. The chart shows your cash running down month by month.

## How the burn rate calculator works

```text
gross burn = monthly costs
net burn   = monthly costs − monthly revenue
runway     = cash ÷ net burn (in months)

highest burn for a target = cash ÷ target months
cut needed                = net burn − highest burn
```

If revenue covers your costs, net burn is zero or less, and your cash isn't running down at all.

## Cash runway calculator: worked examples

### 1. A small company with some revenue

A studio has $120,000 in the bank. It spends $25,000 a month and earns $10,000.

- Net burn: **$15,000.00** a month
- Runway: **8.0 months**
- On costs alone: **4.8 months**

**What this means for the studio:** revenue nearly doubles how long the cash lasts.

### 2. Aiming for 12 months

The same studio wants 12 months of runway.

- Highest burn for 12 months: **$10,000.00**
- Cut needed: **$5,000.00** a month

### 3. No revenue yet

A founder has $50,000 and spends $10,000 a month before launch.

- Runway: **5.0 months**

### 4. A short runway

A team has $100,000 and spends $30,000 a month with no revenue.

- Runway: **3.3 months**, about 3 months and 10 days

**What this means for the team:** it's time to cut costs, find revenue or raise cash.

### 5. A business in rupees

A company has ₹50,00,000, spends ₹8,00,000 a month and earns ₹3,00,000.

- Net burn: **₹5,00,000.00** a month
- Runway: **10.0 months**

## Startup runway calculator tips

- **Use real cash only.** Count money in the bank, not invoices you hope will be paid.
- **Use a typical month.** One-off costs can make a single month look worse or better than usual.
- **Watch net burn, plan on gross burn.** Revenue can drop, so know how long you'd last without it.
- **Check it every month.** Runway changes whenever spending or revenue changes.
- **Know your break-even point.** The <ToolLink slug="break-even-calculator">break even calculator</ToolLink> shows the sales that would stop the burn.
- **Keep your billable time high.** For service businesses, the <ToolLink slug="utilization-rate-calculator">utilization rate calculator</ToolLink> shows how much of your time earns money.
- **Personal runway too.** The <ToolLink slug="emergency-fund-calculator">emergency fund calculator</ToolLink> does the same job for your household.
