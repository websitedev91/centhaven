This CPC calculator works out what each click on your ads costs, from your ad spend and the clicks you got. Or turn it round and see how many clicks a budget buys at the cost per click you expect. Add your conversion rate to see what each sale, lead or sign-up costs you.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Pick a mode.** Find my CPC works out the cost per click from your results. Clicks from a budget plans ahead.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter your **ad spend** and **clicks**, or your **budget** and **cost per click**. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Add a conversion rate** if you know it: the share of clicks that become a sale, lead or sign-up. Leave it at 0 to skip.

The result shows your cost per click, or the clicks your budget buys. With a conversion rate, it also shows conversions and the cost per conversion.

## How to calculate CPC

```text
cost per click        = ad spend ÷ clicks
clicks from a budget  = budget ÷ cost per click   (rounded down)

conversions           = clicks × conversion rate
cost per conversion   = spend (or budget) ÷ conversions
clicks per conversion = 100 ÷ conversion rate (%)
```

You can't buy part of a click, so a budget that works out to 333.33 clicks shows as "about 333 clicks". Conversions are an average, so 13.5 conversions shows as "about 13.5 conversions (on average)".

## Cost per click calculator: worked examples

### 1. A search campaign

A shop spends $500 on ads and gets 250 clicks. 4% of clicks buy something.

- Cost per click: **$2.00**
- Conversions: **10 conversions**
- Cost per conversion: **$50.00**

**What this means for the shop:** about 25 clicks lead to each sale, so each sale costs 25 × $2.00.

### 2. A campaign in rupees

A business spends ₹20,000 and gets 800 clicks, with a 2.5% conversion rate.

- Cost per click: **₹25.00**
- Conversions: **20 conversions**
- Cost per conversion: **₹1,000.00**

## PPC budget calculator: planning ahead

### 3. Clicks from a budget

A marketer has a budget of $1,000 and expects to pay $1.25 a click. They expect 3% of clicks to convert.

- Clicks: **800 clicks**
- Conversions: **24 conversions**
- Cost per conversion: **$41.67**

**What this means:** if the real cost per click is higher, the same budget buys fewer clicks and fewer conversions. Run the numbers again with your actual CPC after the first few days.

## Cost per conversion calculator: why it matters more than CPC

A low cost per click is only good if those clicks turn into customers. Compare two campaigns:

- Campaign A: $1.00 a click, 1% of clicks convert → $100 per conversion
- Campaign B: $2.50 a click, 5% of clicks convert → $50 per conversion

Campaign B's clicks cost more than twice as much, but each sale costs half as much. Use Compare two campaigns under the calculator to put your own campaigns side by side.

## Tips

- **Compare by cost per conversion.** CPC shows what attention costs. Cost per conversion shows what results cost.
- **Use the same dates.** Take spend, clicks and conversions from the same campaign and period.
- **Check the revenue too.** The <ToolLink slug="roas-calculator">ROAS calculator</ToolLink> shows whether the sales cover the ad spend.
- **Follow leads through.** If your conversions are enquiries, the <ToolLink slug="cost-per-lead-calculator">cost per lead calculator</ToolLink> shows what each customer costs.
- **Count every cost of a customer.** The <ToolLink slug="cac-calculator">CAC calculator</ToolLink> adds sales and other marketing costs to the ad spend.
