This hourly to salary calculator turns an hourly wage into yearly, monthly, bi-weekly, weekly and daily pay. It also works the other way: enter a yearly salary and it shows the hourly rate that salary is worth. Every figure is before tax.

Use it to compare a job offer with your current pay, check a contract rate, or see what extra hours are really worth.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose a direction.** "Hourly to salary" starts from a wage. "Salary to hourly" starts from a yearly salary.
2. **Pick your currency.** The starting values change to suit it. Anything you type stays as it is.
3. **Enter your wage or salary.** Use your pay before tax and deductions.
4. **Set your hours and days per week.** Days per week is only used for the daily figure.
5. **Set your paid weeks.** Keep 52 if your holidays are paid. Lower it if you take unpaid time off.
6. **Add overtime if you work it.** Tick "Include overtime", enter the extra hours a week and the pay rate, for example 1.5 for time and a half.

The main result updates as you type. Below it you'll see the full breakdown and a quick table showing the same wage at 30, 35, 40 and 45 hours a week.

## How to convert an hourly wage to an annual salary

Multiply your hourly wage by the hours you work each week, then by the number of weeks you're paid for:

```text
annual salary = hourly wage × hours per week × paid weeks
monthly pay   = annual salary ÷ 12
bi-weekly pay = annual salary ÷ 26
weekly pay    = hourly wage × hours per week
daily pay     = weekly pay ÷ days per week
```

A full-time year of 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 paid hours. That's why people often say "double the hourly wage and add three zeros" for a rough yearly figure: $25 an hour is about $50,000. The exact answer is a little higher, at $52,000.

## How to convert an annual salary to an hourly rate

Going the other way, divide the salary by the hours you're paid for in a year:

```text
hourly wage = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks)
```

A $75,000 salary at 40 hours a week and 52 paid weeks is $75,000 ÷ 2,080 = **$36.06** an hour. If your job really needs 45 hours a week, the same salary is worth less per hour. The quick table under the result shows this straight away.

## Bi-weekly vs semi-monthly pay

The two sound alike but give different paydays. **Bi-weekly** means every two weeks: 26 paychecks a year, so a $52,000 salary is $2,000 a paycheck. **Semi-monthly** means twice a month, usually on fixed dates: 24 paychecks, so the same salary is $2,166.67 a paycheck. The yearly total is the same; each semi-monthly check is just a little larger.

## Freelancing? Your hourly rate must be higher

This calculator is built for employees, where holidays are often paid and the employer covers equipment and part of your taxes. If you work for yourself, you can't simply divide your old salary by 2,080 hours.

Freelancers pay their own costs, take unpaid time off and can't bill every hour they work. To find a rate that really replaces a salary, use the <ToolLink slug="hourly-rate-calculator">freelance hourly rate calculator</ToolLink>. It adds tax, expenses and unpaid time on top of the income you want.

## Tips and common mistakes

- **Use gross pay.** Enter your wage before tax. Comparing a gross salary with a take-home wage makes one offer look far better than it is.
- **Count unpaid time off.** If you don't get paid holidays, lower the paid weeks. Two unpaid weeks cut a year's pay by almost 4%.
- **Check the hours behind a salary.** A higher salary with longer hours can pay less per hour. Switch to "Salary to hourly" to compare offers fairly.
- **Monthly isn't four weeks.** A month is about 4.33 weeks, so monthly pay is the yearly figure divided by 12, not weekly pay times 4.
- **Remember benefits.** Pension contributions, health cover and bonuses add value that a wage alone doesn't show.
