This rule of 72 calculator shows how many years it takes for money to double at a steady yearly rate, or the rate you need to double it in a set number of years. Next to the quick shortcut it shows the exact answer, so you can see how close the rule is. The rate always comes from you; the calculator never suggests one.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose what to work out.** Years to double if you know the rate, or Rate needed if you know how many years you have.
2. **Enter your yearly rate** or your **number of years**. The starting value is an example only, not a suggestion.

The result shows the rule-of-72 answer, the exact answer and the gap between them. There is no amount box: doubling time is the same for any amount.

## Rule of 72 formula

```text
years to double (rule)  = 72 ÷ yearly rate
years to double (exact) = ln 2 ÷ ln(1 + rate ÷ 100)

rate needed (rule)      = 72 ÷ years
rate needed (exact)     = (2 ^ (1 ÷ years) − 1) × 100
```

Both exact answers assume the rate stays the same and growth is added once a year.

## Doubling time calculator: worked examples

### 1. If your rate is 8%

- Rule of 72: 72 ÷ 8 = **9 years**
- Exact answer: **9.01 years**

**What this means:** at a steady 8% a year, the shortcut is almost spot on.

### 2. Doubling in 10 years

- Rule of 72: 72 ÷ 10 = **7.2%** a year
- Exact answer: **7.18%** a year

**What this means:** the rule asks for a touch more than you need, by 0.02 percentage points.

### 3. A very low rate

If your rate is 1% a year:

- Rule of 72: **72 years**
- Exact answer: **69.66 years**

**What this means:** at very low rates the shortcut overestimates the time. At very high rates it goes the other way: at 100% the rule says 0.72 years, but the exact answer is 1 year.

## How long to double my money: what the answer assumes

The answer is a piece of maths, not a forecast. It assumes the same rate every year, growth added once a year, and nothing taken out or added along the way. Real returns go up and down, fees and taxes take their share, and you may pay in more over time. Use the result to compare rates and time frames, not to predict what will happen.

## Years to double calculator: comparing two rates

Use Compare two rates under the calculator to put two rates side by side, for example 6% and 8%. In Rate needed mode, the same button compares two time frames. A small change in the rate makes a big difference to the doubling time: 6% takes 12 years by the rule, while 8% takes 9.
