This meeting cost calculator shows what a meeting costs in people's time. Enter how many people attend, their hourly cost and how long the meeting lasts. Switch to a rate per person if pay differs, and choose Repeats to see what a regular meeting adds up to each week and each year.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose how to enter costs.** Use the same rate for everyone, or a rate per person for a mixed team.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter the **number of people** and their **hourly cost**. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Enter the length** of the meeting in minutes.
4. **Choose Repeats** for a regular meeting, then enter how many times a week it runs and the weeks a year.
5. **Compare two plans** to see what a shorter or smaller meeting would save.

The result shows the cost of the meeting, the cost per minute and the person-hours used, and for a repeat, the weekly and yearly cost.

## How much does a meeting cost?

```text
hourly cost of the room = people × hourly cost
                          (or the sum of everyone's hourly cost)
meeting cost  = hourly cost of the room × minutes ÷ 60
cost per minute = hourly cost of the room ÷ 60

weekly cost = meeting cost × meetings per week
yearly cost = weekly cost × weeks a year
```

The hourly cost is your own number. Use hourly pay, or pay plus other employment costs if you know them.

## Recurring meeting cost: why repeats add up

A single meeting rarely looks expensive. The cost shows when it repeats. A small saving on one meeting turns into a big one over a year. Fifteen minutes off a weekly meeting for eight people at $75 an hour saves $150 a week, which is $7,200 over 48 weeks.

## Meeting cost per year: tips

- **Invite only who is needed.** Each extra person adds their hourly cost to every minute.
- **Set a shorter default.** Try 25 or 45 minutes instead of 30 or 60.
- **Send notes instead.** Updates that need no discussion can be a message.
- **Review repeats every quarter.** Ask whether each regular meeting still needs to happen.
- **Know your hourly figures.** The <ToolLink slug="hourly-to-salary-calculator">hourly to salary calculator</ToolLink> turns a salary into an hourly cost.
- **Protect billable time.** The <ToolLink slug="utilization-rate-calculator">utilization rate calculator</ToolLink> shows how much of your time earns money.
- **Keep costs in check.** The <ToolLink slug="break-even-calculator">break even calculator</ToolLink> shows how many sales cover your fixed costs.
