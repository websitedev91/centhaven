This bonus calculator works out a gross bonus three ways: as a percent of your salary, as a fixed amount, or as a target percent multiplied by how much of your goal you achieved. It shows the bonus, your total pay for the year and, if the bonus is spread out, how much it adds to each paycheck. Every number comes from you, and it never estimates tax.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose the bonus type.** % of salary for a set percentage, Fixed amount for a sum you were told, or Target × achievement for a bonus that depends on results.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter your **annual salary** before tax.
3. **Enter the bonus**: the percentage, the amount, or your target percentage and your achievement (payout) percentage.
4. **Spread it, if needed.** Enter the number of paychecks if your bonus is split across them. Leave the box empty for one lump sum.

The starting values are examples only. Use the numbers from your contract or bonus plan.

## How to calculate bonus percentage of salary

```text
% of salary:           bonus = salary × bonus % ÷ 100
fixed amount:          bonus % of salary = amount ÷ salary × 100
target × achievement:  bonus = salary × target % × achievement % ÷ 10,000

total pay              = salary + bonus
bonus per paycheck     = bonus ÷ paychecks
```

## Target bonus calculator: worked examples

### 1. A percentage of salary

A 10% bonus on a $60,000 salary.

- Bonus: 60,000 × 10% = **$6,000.00**
- Total pay for the year: **$66,000.00**

**What this means:** before tax, the bonus adds a tenth of your salary to your year.

### 2. A target with 120% achievement

A 10% target on a $60,000 salary, with 120% of the target paid out.

- Bonus: 60,000 × 10% × 120% = **$7,200.00**
- That is **12%** of salary
- Total pay for the year: **$67,200.00**

**What this means:** beating your goal by a fifth lifts the bonus by a fifth, from $6,000 to $7,200, if your plan pays in a straight line.

### 3. In rupees, spread over 26 paychecks

A 10% target on ₹12,00,000, with 120% achievement, spread over 26 paychecks.

- Bonus: **₹1,44,000.00**
- Total pay: **₹13,44,000.00**
- Bonus per paycheck: **₹5,538.46**
- Pay per paycheck with the bonus: **₹51,692.31**

## Annual bonus calculator: fixed amounts as a percentage

If you were offered a fixed bonus, the calculator shows it as a share of your salary so you can compare it with a percentage offer. A $5,000 bonus on $60,000 is **8.33%** of salary, and total pay is $65,000. A £3,000 bonus on £40,000 is **7.5%**.

## Gross, not take-home

Every figure here is before tax and deductions. How much you receive depends on your tax situation and how your employer pays bonuses, and we don’t estimate either. Your payslip will show the amount after deductions.
