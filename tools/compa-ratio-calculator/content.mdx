This compa ratio calculator shows where a salary sits in its pay range. Enter the salary and the range minimum and maximum, or your employer's own midpoint, to see the compa ratio, the midpoint and the range penetration. It uses only the numbers you enter: it never looks up market pay data.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose where the midpoint comes from.** The range works it out halfway between the minimum and maximum. My own midpoint uses a figure your employer publishes.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter the **salary**: yours, an offer or an employee's. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Enter the range minimum and maximum** from the pay band or job posting, or the **midpoint** if you have it.

The result shows the compa ratio as a number and a percentage, the midpoint, the difference from the midpoint and the range penetration, with a bar showing where the salary sits.

## How to calculate compa ratio

```text
midpoint          = (range minimum + range maximum) ÷ 2
compa ratio       = salary ÷ midpoint
range penetration = (salary − minimum) ÷ (maximum − minimum) × 100
```

A compa ratio of 1.00 means the salary is exactly at the midpoint. Below 1.00 is below it, and above 1.00 is above it.

## Salary midpoint calculator: worked examples

### 1. Inside the range

A salary of $72,000 sits in a range of $60,000 to $90,000.

- Midpoint: **$75,000.00**
- Compa ratio: **0.96**, or 96% of the midpoint
- Range penetration: **40%**
- $3,000 below the midpoint

**What this means:** the salary is a little under the middle of the range, and 40% of the way from the bottom to the top.

### 2. Above the range

A salary of $95,000 sits in the same $60,000 to $90,000 range.

- Compa ratio: **1.27**
- Range penetration: **116.67%**, above the top of the range

**What this means:** a penetration over 100% means the salary is above the published maximum. Some employers allow that; the calculator only shows the position.

### 3. In rupees

A salary of ₹12,00,000 sits in a range of ₹10,00,000 to ₹15,00,000.

- Midpoint: **₹12,50,000.00**
- Compa ratio: **0.96**
- Range penetration: **40%**

## Range penetration calculator: why it adds to the ratio

The compa ratio only compares a salary with the middle of the range. Range penetration shows how far through the whole range it is. In a narrow range, a small raise moves the penetration a lot; in a wide range, the same raise moves it a little. Use Compare two salaries under the calculator to see how a raise changes both numbers.

## Salary range position calculator: using it fairly

Use the same pay period for every number: all yearly, all monthly or all hourly. Check that the range you enter is the one for this role and grade. The calculator shows where a salary sits; it does not say where it should be. That depends on the employer's pay policy, experience and performance.
