This conversion rate calculator works out what share of your visitors take the action you want: a sale, a lead or a sign-up. You can also work backwards, to see how many conversions a target rate means or how many visitors you need to reach a goal. Add what one conversion is worth to see the revenue.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Pick a mode.** Find my rate works out your rate from your results. Conversions needed and Visitors needed plan ahead from a target rate.
2. **Enter your visitors and conversions**, or your target rate and goal. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Add a value per conversion** if you know it, such as your average order value. Leave it at 0 to skip.

The result shows your conversion rate, conversions or visitors needed, how many visitors it takes for each conversion and, with a value, the revenue and revenue per visitor.

## How to calculate conversion rate

```text
conversion rate     = conversions ÷ visitors × 100
conversions needed  = visitors × target rate ÷ 100
visitors needed     = conversions goal ÷ (target rate ÷ 100)   (rounded up)

revenue             = conversions × value per conversion
revenue per visitor = revenue ÷ visitors
```

Use visitors, sessions or clicks, as long as you count them the same way as your conversions.

## Website conversion rate calculator: worked examples

### 1. An online shop

A shop had 5,000 visitors last month and 150 of them bought something. An average order is worth $50.

- Conversion rate: **3%**
- About 33.3 visitors for each sale
- Revenue: **$7,500.00**
- Revenue per visitor: **$1.50**

**What this means for the shop:** every visitor is worth $1.50 on average, which is useful when deciding what to pay for traffic.

### 2. A shop in rupees

A store had 3,000 visitors and 90 orders, each worth ₹2,000 on average.

- Conversion rate: **3%**
- Revenue: **₹1,80,000.00**
- Revenue per visitor: **₹60.00**

## Conversions needed calculator: planning ahead

### 3. What a target rate means

The shop expects 5,000 visitors next month and wants a 4% conversion rate.

- Conversions at the target: **200 conversions**
- Revenue at $50 each: **$10,000.00**

### 4. Visitors for a goal

To reach 200 sales at a 3% rate, the shop needs 200 ÷ 0.03 = 6,666.67 visitors.

- Visitors needed: **6,667 visitors** (rounded up)

**What this means:** raising the rate from 3% to 4% would reach the same 200 sales with 5,000 visitors, so improving the page can be worth as much as buying more traffic.

## Ecommerce conversion rate calculator: rate and revenue together

A higher rate is not always more money. A page that converts at 4% with $30 orders earns $1.20 per visitor, while one at 3% with $50 orders earns $1.50. Use Compare two pages under the calculator, add your value per conversion, and look at revenue per visitor as well as the rate.
