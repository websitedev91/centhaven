This unit price calculator compares up to four items by what they cost per unit, so you can see which is really cheapest. Enter each item’s price and size, in grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, millilitres, litres, fluid ounces or a count, and it converts them to one unit and shows how much more the others cost. Every price and size comes from you.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose your currency** and how to **show the price**: per 100 g, per kg, per oz or per lb for weights; per 100 ml, per litre or per fl oz for volumes; per item for counts.
2. **Enter each item’s price and size**, with the unit printed on its label. For a multipack, enter the total: 6 × 330 ml is 1,980 ml.
3. **Add items** to compare up to four. Use the same kind of unit for all of them: weights with weights, volumes with volumes.

The starting values are examples only.

## Price per unit calculator: the formula

```text
price per base unit = price ÷ (size × unit size in g, ml or items)
shown price         = price per base unit × the unit you show it in
% more              = (item − cheapest) ÷ cheapest × 100
```

The unit sizes are fixed definitions: 1 kg = 1,000 g, 1 lb = 453.59237 g, 1 oz = 28.349523125 g, 1 l = 1,000 ml, 1 fl oz (US) = 29.5735295625 ml and 1 fl oz (UK) = 28.4130625 ml.

## Price per ounce calculator: worked examples

### 1. Two jar sizes

Item 1 is $4.99 for 16 oz. Item 2 is $7.49 for 32 oz.

- Item 1: **$0.31** per oz
- Item 2: **$0.23** per oz
- Cheapest: **Item 2**; Item 1 costs **33.24% more** per unit

**What this means:** the bigger jar saves about a quarter on every ounce, if you will use it all.

### 2. Different units

Item 1 is $3.00 for 1 lb. Item 2 is $6.00 for 1 kg.

- Item 1: **$6.61** per kg
- Item 2: **$6.00** per kg
- Item 1 costs **10.23% more** per unit

**What this means:** the pound pack looks cheaper on the shelf, but it holds less than half a kilogram.

### 3. A small can and a big bottle

Item 1 is $1.80 for 1.5 l. Item 2 is $0.65 for 330 ml.

- Item 1: **$1.20** per litre
- Item 2: **$1.97** per litre, **64.14% more**

## Price per kg calculator: metric packs

In grams, £2.50 for 500 g is **£0.50 per 100 g**, while £4.20 for 1 kg is **£0.42 per 100 g**, so the small pack costs 19.05% more per unit. In rupees, ₹120 for 500 g is ₹24.00 per 100 g and ₹210 for 1 kg is ₹21.00.

## Which is cheaper calculator: when unit price isn’t everything

A lower unit price only saves money if you use what you buy. A big pack that goes off before you finish it can cost more in the end. Storage space, how fast you use something and whether you need it at all matter too. The calculator shows the maths; you decide what fits your home.
