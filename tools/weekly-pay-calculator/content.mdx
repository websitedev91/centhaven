This weekly pay calculator turns an annual salary or an hourly wage into gross pay every week. Add overtime at your own overtime rate, and see what that adds up to over a year, every two weeks and as a monthly average. It shows gross pay only: the amount before tax and deductions.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Pick a mode.** From a salary splits a yearly amount into weeks. From an hourly wage builds it up from your rate and hours.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter your **annual salary**, or your **hourly rate** and **hours per week**. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Add overtime** if you have it: the extra hours in the week and the overtime rate you are actually paid. Leave them at 0 to skip.

The result shows your weekly gross pay, your yearly gross, a 53-payday year, pay every two weeks and the monthly average.

## Weekly salary calculator: the formula

```text
from a salary:    weekly = salary ÷ 52
                  53-payday split = salary ÷ 53

from an hourly wage:
                  weekly = rate × hours per week
                           + overtime hours × overtime rate
                  yearly = weekly × 52
                  53-payday year = weekly × 53

both:             every two weeks = weekly × 2
                  monthly average = yearly ÷ 12
```

A year has 52 weeks and 1 or 2 extra days, so weekly paydays fall 52 times in most years and 53 in some. Ask your employer how they handle a 53rd payday: the calculator shows both results.

## Worked examples

### 1. A yearly salary

Someone earns $52,000 a year, paid every week.

- Weekly gross pay: **$1,000.00**
- If the salary is split over 53 paydays: **$981.13** each
- Every two weeks: **$2,000.00**

**What this means:** in a 53-payday year, a salary split 53 ways gives a slightly smaller payment each week, but the year adds up to the same.

## Hourly to weekly pay

### 2. An hourly wage with overtime

Someone earns $25 an hour for 40 hours a week, plus 5 hours of overtime at an overtime rate of $37.50.

- Overtime: **$187.50** a week (5 × 37.50)
- Weekly gross pay: **$1,187.50** (25 × 40 + 187.50)
- Yearly gross, if every week looks like this: **$61,750.00**

**What this means:** overtime that happens every week adds up quickly. If it only happens some weeks, work out a normal week and an overtime week separately.

### 3. A salary in rupees

Someone earns ₹12,00,000 a year.

- Weekly gross pay: **₹23,076.92**
- Monthly average: **₹1,00,000.00**

## How much is my weekly pay after a raise?

Change the salary or the hourly rate to the new figure and the weekly pay updates at once. A raise from $25 to $27 an hour at 40 hours adds $80 a week, or $4,160 over 52 weeks. The pay raise calculator works out the new figure from a percentage.

## Tips

- **Compare on gross pay.** Your payslip shows less after tax and deductions. Compare jobs on gross pay, then check the take-home with your employer.
- **Plan on your normal week.** Treat overtime that comes and goes as a bonus, not as part of your budget.
