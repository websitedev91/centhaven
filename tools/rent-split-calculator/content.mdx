This rent split calculator shares the rent and shared bills between 2 to 6 people. Split it equally, by the size of each person’s room, or by each person’s income, and see what everyone pays each month. The shares always add up to the rent exactly, and every number comes from you.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose how to split the rent**: Equally, By room size or By income.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter the **monthly rent** for the whole home.
3. **Add shared bills** such as utilities and internet, if you want them in the split. Leave the box at 0 for none.
4. **Add the people.** Up to six, called Person 1 to Person 6. For room size, enter each room’s size; for income, each person’s income for the same period.
5. **By room size only:** if you want part of the rent shared equally for the kitchen and living room, enter that share as a percentage.

The starting values are examples only.

## How the shares are worked out

```text
equally:       each share = rent ÷ number of people
by room size:  each share = shared space ÷ people
                          + (rent − shared space) × room size ÷ all room sizes
by income:     each share = rent × income ÷ all incomes
bills:         equally, or the same way as the rent
```

Each share is rounded down to the cent, then any leftover cents go to Person 1, Person 2 and so on, so the total is always exact.

## Split rent by room size: worked examples

### 1. Equally

Three people share $2,400 rent and $300 of bills.

- Rent: **$800.00** each
- Bills: **$100.00** each
- Total: **$900.00** each

### 2. By room size, with shared space

Rooms are 150, 120 and 90 square feet. Rent is $2,400, and 25% of it is for shared space.

- Shared space: $600, so **$200.00** each
- The other $1,800 by room size: **$750.00 / $600.00 / $450.00**
- Rent to pay: **$950.00 / $800.00 / $650.00**

**What this means:** the biggest room still pays the most, but everyone pays the same for the rooms you all use. With no shared space, the same rooms pay $1,000, $800 and $600.

### 3. Rounding to the cent

Three people split $1,000 rent equally.

- Shares: **$333.34 / $333.33 / $333.33**

**What this means:** $1,000 doesn’t divide into three whole cents, so Person 1 takes the extra cent and the total is exactly $1,000.

## Split rent by income: worked example

Three people earn $4,000, $3,000 and $3,000 a month, so their income shares are 40%, 30% and 30%. On $2,400 rent they pay **$960.00, $720.00 and $720.00**. In rupees, ₹30,000 rent split by incomes of ₹60,000, ₹45,000 and ₹45,000 is **₹12,000.00, ₹9,000.00 and ₹9,000.00**.

## Roommate rent calculator: choosing a method

Each method weighs something different. Equal splits are simplest. Room size weighs private space. Income weighs what each person can put in. None is right for every home, so try all three with your own numbers and agree together. The calculator only shows the maths.
