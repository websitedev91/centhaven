This savings goal calculator shows how much to save each month to reach a goal by the date you choose. Enter the goal, what you've already saved and how many months you have. You can also add the interest rate on your savings account, if it pays one.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose your currency** and enter your **savings goal**. The starting goal is an example only.
2. **Enter what you've already saved.** Leave it at 0 if you're starting from nothing.
3. **Enter the number of months** until you want to reach the goal.
4. **Add an interest rate** if your savings earn interest. Use your own account's rate, or leave it at 0.

The result shows the amount to save each month, what you'll put in, any interest earned, and the month you'll reach your goal. The chart shows your savings growing month by month.

## How much to save per month: the formula

Without interest, the maths is simple:

```text
monthly saving = (goal − already saved) ÷ months
```

With interest added monthly at your yearly rate ÷ 12 (r), your current savings grow too, and each deposit earns interest for the months after it:

```text
grown savings  = already saved × (1 + r)^months
monthly saving = (goal − grown savings) × r ÷ ((1 + r)^months − 1)
```

The calculator assumes you save at the end of each month.

**What this means for Lena:** about $96 a week gets her there on time.

Ravi wants $10,000 in 12 months and already has $2,000.

- Save each month: **$666.67**

**What this means for Ravi:** his head start saves him $166.67 a month compared with starting from nothing.

- Interest earned: **$1,815.72**
