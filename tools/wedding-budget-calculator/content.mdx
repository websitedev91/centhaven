This wedding budget calculator splits your own total budget across eight parts of the day, from venue and catering to stationery, using percentages you set. It also shows the cost per guest for your guest list. The starting split is an example to change, not an average: every number comes from you.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose your currency** and enter your **total budget**.
2. **Enter your guest count**, if you want the cost per guest. Leave it at 0 to skip it.
3. **Change the example split.** Give each category your own percentage, set any you don’t need to 0%, and keep the total at exactly 100%.

The starting values are examples only. If the total isn’t 100%, the calculator tells you, and Reset to the example split puts the starting percentages back.

## How to split a wedding budget

```text
category amount  = total budget × category % ÷ 100   (all % together = 100)
cost per guest   = total budget ÷ guests
venue per guest  = venue & catering amount ÷ guests
```

Each category is rounded down to the cent, then any leftover cents go to the first categories, so the eight amounts always add up to your budget exactly.

## Wedding budget breakdown: worked examples

### 1. The example split

A total budget of $25,000 for 100 guests.

- Venue & catering (45%): **$11,250.00**
- Photography & video (12%): **$3,000.00**
- Flowers & decor (10%) and Other & buffer (10%): **$2,500.00** each
- Attire & beauty (8%): **$2,000.00**
- Music & entertainment (7%): **$1,750.00**
- Rings (5%): **$1,250.00**
- Stationery (3%): **$750.00**
- Cost per guest: **$250.00**

**What this means:** with this split, nearly half the budget goes on the venue and food, which is $112.50 for each guest.

### 2. In rupees

A budget of ₹15,00,000 for 150 guests with the same split.

- Venue & catering: **₹6,75,000.00**
- Cost per guest: **₹10,000.00**

### 3. Your own split, with decimals

A budget of $1,234.56 with venue and catering at 45.5% and photography at 11.5%. The amounts come to **$561.73** and **$141.98**, and all eight add up to exactly $1,234.56.

## Wedding cost per guest calculator: the guest list

The guest list often moves the budget more than anything else, because food, drink and seating grow with every name. Use Compare two guest counts under the calculator to see how 100 guests against 120 changes the cost per guest. The same budget spread across more people leaves less for each one.

## Wedding budget planner: making the split yours

Every wedding is different, so start from what matters most to you. If photos are your priority, give them more and take it from somewhere you care about less. If a relative is covering the flowers, set that to 0%. Keep some money in Other & buffer for things you haven’t thought of yet.
