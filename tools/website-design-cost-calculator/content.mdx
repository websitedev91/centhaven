This website design cost calculator estimates a web project from the hours it needs and your own hourly rate. Pick the number of pages, a design level and the features, adjust the example hours to match how you work, and see the project price, a quote range and an optional monthly maintenance fee.

## How to use this web design pricing calculator

1. **Pick your currency** and enter **your hourly rate**. Not sure what it should be? Work it out first with the <ToolLink slug="hourly-rate-calculator">freelance hourly rate calculator</ToolLink>.
2. **Enter the number of pages** and the design and build hours for a typical page.
3. **Choose a design level.** Template halves the page hours, Custom uses them as entered and Premium adds half again.
4. **Set the hours for setup, discovery and launch:** calls, planning, hosting, testing and going live.
5. **Tick the features the site needs** and adjust their hours. Copywriting is counted per page.
6. **Set a buffer** for feedback rounds and project management.
7. **Optional: add maintenance hours** for each month after launch.

The main result is the project price including the buffer. Below it you'll see the quote range, the total hours and the maintenance fee, then a breakdown of hours and price for each part of the project.

All hours are example values. They are not market prices: change them to match how you work.

## How the estimate is calculated

```text
design hours   = pages × hours per page × design level   (template 0.5, custom 1, premium 1.5)
copy hours     = pages × copywriting hours per page      (if ticked)
feature hours  = sum of the hours for each ticked feature
base hours     = setup + design hours + copy hours + feature hours
total hours    = base hours × (1 + buffer)
project price  = total hours × your hourly rate
quote range    = base hours × rate  to  total hours × rate
maintenance    = maintenance hours × rate, per month
```

## How much to charge for a website

Price the project, not the hours. Use the hours to work out a fair price, then quote one fixed price for a clear list of deliverables. Clients compare totals, and a fixed price rewards you for working efficiently.

The buffer matters more than it looks. Feedback rounds, emails, waiting for content and small changes all take time that never appears on a feature list. If you've ever finished a project and felt underpaid, a bigger buffer is usually the fix.

## Website cost estimate: what changes the price

- **Pages:** more unique layouts mean more design and build time.
- **Design level:** a tested template is quicker than a custom design from a blank page.
- **Features:** an online shop, bookings or several languages each add a lot of work.
- **Content:** writing the copy yourself adds hours for every page.
- **Your rate:** two freelancers with the same scope can quote very different prices.

## Worked examples

### 1. A US freelancer's 5-page small-business site

Sam charges $75 an hour. The client needs 5 pages with a custom design, a contact form and SEO setup. Sam uses 4 hours per page, 6 hours for setup and launch, a 15% buffer and 2 hours of maintenance a month.

- Project price: **$2,846.25** (37.95 hours)
- Quote range: from **$2,475.00** without the buffer
- Maintenance: **$150.00** a month, **$1,800.00** a year

**What this means for Sam:** a quote of about $2,850 covers the work and the back-and-forth. The maintenance plan adds steady income after launch.

### 2. An Indian agency's 10-page premium site in two languages

The agency charges ₹1,500 an hour. The site has 10 pages at premium design level, copywriting at 2 hours per page, a contact form, a blog, SEO setup and a second language. They add a 20% buffer and 4 hours of maintenance a month.

- Project price: **₹1,96,200.00** (130.8 hours)
- Quote range: from **₹1,63,500.00** without the buffer
- Maintenance: **₹6,000.00** a month, **₹72,000.00** a year

**What this means for the agency:** the premium design and the second language make up more than half the hours, so those are the first things to discuss if the client's budget is lower.

### 3. A UK designer's template shop

Priya charges £60 an hour for a 3-page template site with an online shop and a contact form, 6 hours of setup and no buffer.

- Project price: **£2,340.00** (39 hours)

**What this means for Priya:** the shop is 25 of the 39 hours. With no buffer, any extra feedback round comes out of her own pocket, so a 10–15% buffer would be safer.

## Tips and common mistakes

- **Track your real hours** on each project and update the example values.
- **Write down the number of revision rounds** in the quote, and charge for extra ones.
- **Charge for content delays.** Set a date for the client's content and say what happens if it's late.
- **Offer maintenance as a monthly plan.** Price a bigger plan with the <ToolLink slug="retainer-fee-calculator">retainer fee calculator</ToolLink>.
