This part time salary calculator turns a full-time salary into what you earn for fewer hours, or works out yearly pay from an hourly wage. Enter the full-time salary, the full-time hours for the job and your own hours, and it shows your yearly, monthly and weekly pay and your share of full time. The full-time hours come from you, because every employer sets its own.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Choose how you’re paid.** From a full-time salary for a pro rata job, or From an hourly wage if you are paid by the hour.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter the **full-time annual salary**, or your **hourly rate**.
3. **Enter the hours.** For pro rata, the full-time hours per week for the job (40 is an example only) and your part-time hours. For hourly, your hours per week and the paid weeks per year.

The starting values are examples only. Check your contract or job ad for the real figures.

## How to calculate part time salary

```text
from a full-time salary:  share of full time = part-time hours ÷ full-time hours
                          part-time salary   = full-time salary × share
                          hourly equivalent  = full-time salary ÷ (full-time hours × 52)
from an hourly wage:      weekly             = hourly rate × hours per week
                          yearly             = weekly × paid weeks
both:                     monthly average    = yearly ÷ 12
```

## Pro rata salary calculator: worked examples

### 1. Three days of a five-day week

The full-time job pays $50,000 for 40 hours a week. You work 24 hours.

- Share of full time: 24 ÷ 40 = **60%**
- Part-time salary: **$30,000.00** a year
- Monthly average: **$2,500.00**
- Weekly: **$576.92**
- Hourly equivalent: **$24.04**

**What this means:** you earn 60% of the full-time salary for 60% of the hours, at the same hourly rate.

### 2. Paid by the hour

You earn $20 an hour for 20 hours a week, for 52 weeks.

- Weekly: **$400.00**
- Yearly: **$20,800.00**
- Monthly average: **$1,733.33**

**What this means:** if some weeks are unpaid, lower the paid weeks. At 46 weeks and £15 for 16 hours, the year comes to **£11,040.00**.

### 3. In rupees

The full-time job pays ₹6,00,000 for 40 hours. You work 20 hours.

- Share of full time: **50%**
- Part-time salary: **₹3,00,000.00**
- Monthly average: **₹25,000.00**

## FTE salary calculator: reading the share of full time

Employers often describe part-time jobs in FTE, full-time equivalent. The share of full time in the result is the same idea as a percentage: 0.6 FTE is 60%, 0.5 FTE is 50%. Multiply the full-time salary by the share and you have the FTE salary. Because full-time weeks differ, from 35 to 40 hours or more, always use the full-time hours for your own job.
