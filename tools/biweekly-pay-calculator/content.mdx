This biweekly pay calculator turns an annual salary or an hourly wage into gross pay every two weeks. It also shows what a 27-payday year looks like, what you would get if you were paid twice a month instead, and your monthly and weekly averages. It shows gross pay only: the amount before tax and deductions.

## How to use the calculator

1. **Pick a mode.** From a salary splits a yearly amount. From an hourly wage builds it up from your rate and hours.
2. **Choose your currency** and enter your **annual salary**, or your **hourly rate** and **hours per week**. The starting values are examples only.
3. **Add overtime** if you have it: the extra hours across the two weeks and your own overtime rate. Leave them at 0 to skip.

The result shows your biweekly gross pay, your yearly gross, the 27-payday figure, twice-a-month pay, the monthly average and weekly pay.

## Biweekly salary calculator: the formula

```text
from a salary:   biweekly = salary ÷ 26
                 27-payday split = salary ÷ 27

from an hourly wage:
                 biweekly = rate × hours per week × 2
                            + overtime hours × overtime rate
                 yearly   = biweekly × 26
                 27-payday year = biweekly × 27

both:            twice a month = yearly ÷ 24
                 monthly average = yearly ÷ 12
                 weekly = biweekly ÷ 2
```

A year has 52 weeks and 1 or 2 extra days, so paydays every two weeks fall 26 times in most years and 27 in some. Ask your employer how they handle a 27th payday: the calculator shows both results.

## Worked examples

### 1. A yearly salary

Someone earns $52,000 a year, paid every two weeks.

- Biweekly gross pay: **$2,000.00**
- If the salary is split over 27 paydays: **$1,925.93** each
- Paid twice a month instead: **$2,166.67**
- Monthly average: **$4,333.33**

**What this means:** in a 27-payday year, a salary split 27 ways gives a slightly smaller payment each time, but the year adds up to the same.

## Hourly to biweekly pay

### 2. An hourly wage

Someone earns $25 an hour and works 40 hours a week.

- Biweekly gross pay: **$2,000.00** (25 × 40 × 2)
- Yearly gross: **$52,000.00**
- In a 27-payday year: **$54,000.00**

**What this means:** hourly pay follows the hours you work, so an extra payday adds a full two weeks of pay to that year.

With 4 hours of overtime per pay period at an overtime rate of $37.50, overtime adds $150.00, so the pay becomes $2,150.00 every two weeks.

### 3. A salary in rupees

Someone earns ₹12,00,000 a year.

- Biweekly gross pay: **₹46,153.85**
- Paid twice a month instead: **₹50,000.00**
- Monthly average: ₹1,00,000.00

## Biweekly vs semi-monthly pay

Biweekly means every 14 days: 26 paydays in most years, and the date moves around the month. Semi-monthly means twice a month on set dates: 24 paydays a year. For the same yearly pay, each semi-monthly payment is bigger, but there are fewer of them. Two months a year with biweekly pay have three paydays instead of two, which can help with planning.
